# Episode 1 storyboard and cue sheet

Logline: A teacher explains the laws of reflection and the key parts of spherical mirrors using a spoon, a simple ray diagram, and a clean sequence of visual definitions that build from everyday observation to textbook vocabulary.

World: a classroom/green-board lesson with a teacher framed on the left and clean 2D diagrams and object inserts on the right; dark navy/charcoal panels keep the board and diagrams readable without relying on green as a teaching colour.

Motif: the mirror lesson begins with a spoon and grows into a mirror vocabulary diagram, then a spherical mirror summary that ends with a quick recap using the same labelled parts: P, F, C, principal axis, aperture, and R = 2f.

Clawd's arc: this is a teacher-led lesson rather than a character arc, so the core focus is clarity and pacing: start with surprise, then build understanding, then close with a clean recap and a bridge into the next episode.

## Graphics to make for Episode 1

1. Reflection vocabulary diagram: flat mirror, incident ray, point of incidence, normal, reflected ray, i and r.
2. Laws of reflection card: i = r and coplanar-ray law.
3. Spherical-mirror profiles: accurate concave/convex profiles with reflecting side and shaded back.
4. Mirror-parts diagram: P, C, principal axis, R and aperture; separate concave/convex versions.
5. Focus animation pair: converging rays to real F for a concave mirror; diverging rays and dotted backward extensions to virtual F for a convex mirror.
6. Focal-length relation card: PF = f, PC = R, R = 2f for small-aperture spherical mirrors.
7. Observation card and recap card: phone-legible, minimal text.

## Keep out of Episode 1

- Do not build a full draggable mirror simulator yet; it adds effort without helping the definition lesson.
- Do not put every definition on one crowded diagram at once; reveal terms progressively and use the recap diagram only after each has been taught.
- Do not demonstrate focusing sunlight; that safety-sensitive activity belongs in Episode 2 as a labelled simulation/animation only.
- Do not use copied trending reel audio, another creator’s meme image, or their distinctive graphics; original short reaction cards are enough.

## Export / review checklist

- Teacher and labels do not overlap; nothing important is behind the teacher.
- All diagrams remain readable on a phone preview.
- i and r arcs visibly start from the normal, not the mirror surface.
- Convex-mirror backward extensions are dotted and do not imply light travels behind the mirror.
- The R = 2f statement is explicitly limited to small-aperture spherical mirrors.
- Live spoon reflections are not pointed into the camera or a bright light.
- Captions are reviewed against the Hinglish voice track; English definitions retain exact spelling.
- Do one rough edit and time it before polishing graphics; trim repeated holds rather than speeding up definitions.
